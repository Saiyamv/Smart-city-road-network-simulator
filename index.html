How the route is found

The city is a graph: each place is a point and each road is a line with a distance. The search starts at your location, always moves to the closest place it has not visited yet, and keeps a note of how it got there. When it reaches your destination, it follows those notes backwards to build the route.

This sample city has five places and five roads. Distances are in kilometres.
